# Content Strategy and Copy Guide

## One voice, five modes

| Mode | Character | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haven | Warm, playful, lightly chaotic | Arrival, community, navigation | Forced whimsy in every sentence |
| Big sister | Protective, teasing, direct, encouraging | Boundaries, reassurance, safety | Infantilizing readers or performative toughness |
| Education | Patient, specific, curious | Nails, resources, process | Medical authority, absolute safety claims, credential inflation |
| Professional | Concise, competent, personable | Brand fit, collaboration, media kit | Corporate jargon or pretending Nari is a different person |
| Support | Grateful, unmistakably optional | Throne, sharing, financial boundaries | Guilt, urgency, donor hierarchy, access promises |

Swearing can be part of Nari's personality, but it should be deliberate and audience-aware. The approved protective quote is a tonal reference, not default homepage or sponsor copy.

## Voice principles

1. **Invite; do not funnel.** “Come in” beats “Convert now.”
2. **Name the real state.** “The gallery is waiting for Nari's approved work” beats fake samples.
3. **Be warm with a spine.** Kindness includes boundaries and does not suppress valid criticism.
4. **Specificity creates personality.** Pajamas, pillow forts, nail polish, yapping, fall light, and game panic are useful; generic “content creator journey” is not.
5. **Humor never changes the contract.** A joke cannot obscure consent, money, privacy, safety, or external navigation.

## Identity copy boundary

Safe now:

- “Nari, your chaotic big-sister VTuber.”
- VTuber, gamer, Haven keeper, self-taught nail artist/content creator.
- Warm, protective, playful, curious, and a prolific yapper.

Blocked until approved:

- Any explanation merging or replacing CatDog and Grim Reaper lore.
- Legal name, location, private family/relationship information, age/birthday, or offline employment.
- “Licensed,” “certified,” “professional nail technician,” “salon,” “clients,” “bookings,” or “services.”
- Medical/sanitation guarantees or advice presented as clinical truth.

## CTA language

Use verbs that describe a real destination: “Catch a stream,” “Open the studio,” “Find the common room,” “Watch the moment,” “Explore the fit,” “Use the key.” External links must add visually hidden or visible “opens in a new tab” context when target behavior does.

Avoid: “Learn more” repeated everywhere, “Join the family” before values, “Donate now,” “Buy me this,” “Limited time,” “Exclusive access,” and fake-form “Submit” actions.

## Community and Discord

Community copy must explicitly include human dignity, respect, care, cooperation, boundaries, and room for different energy levels. “Positive vibes only” is banned because it can erase real concerns.

Discord is described as an entrance deeper in the Haven. It is not exclusive status, a hidden security mechanism, or proof of intimacy with Nari. Reverify the selected invite immediately before launch.

## Nail and education copy

- Identify firsthand practice as firsthand practice.
- Separate Nari's personal process from general instruction.
- Credit sources for technical claims.
- For allergy, infection, chemical, or nail-health topics, use a clear scope statement and direct health questions to an appropriate qualified professional.
- Product copy states why Nari uses/likes it, who it may suit, when it was reviewed, and any material relationship.
- Never turn generated studio art into a portfolio example.

## Professional copy

Lead with brand/creator fit, content modes, working values, and review process. Metrics require source, platform, date, and measurement window. Prior partnerships require permission and current status. “Sponsor-safe” does not mean sterile; it means accurate, rights-cleared, and easy to evaluate.

There is no approved business inbox or form. The current page may point to Linktree/X as public routing surfaces but must not promise response time or a formal intake process.

## Support copy

Required meaning, subject to Nari's final wording:

> Your presence in the Haven already matters. Watching, sharing, or simply being here counts. Financial support is never required, and it does not create ownership, obligation, special access, or a claim on Nari's time or boundaries.

Do not call supporters “better,” list donor tiers as status, imply hardship, run countdowns, or place financial CTAs above the optionality statement.

## Affiliate/resource disclosure

Short disclosure pattern:

> If a link is affiliated, it will be labelled. Nari may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. A relationship never replaces her actual opinion.

Record jurisdiction-specific legal wording separately once counsel/owner approves it. Never label a link affiliated without confirming the account/relationship, and never omit a known material connection.

## Stories and community media

Every story record needs an explicit publication status and privacy review. Do not infer that a stream remark, Discord message, family anecdote, or private chat is approved for permanent indexing. Chat/community screenshots require permission or a defensible public-use basis, redaction of unnecessary identifiers, moderation review, alt text/summary, and a removal path.

Adult-marked VOD content is opt-in and labelled before navigation. Do not surface home/moving/private-person footage simply because it is publicly reachable.

## Placeholder policy

Allowed placeholders are honest system states or original environment/decorative art clearly labelled as noncanonical. Banned placeholders include lorem ipsum, fake testimonials, fake sponsors, fake stats, invented schedule/events, stock/generated nail work presented as Nari's, and generated character art presented as Nari.

Before removing “pending/curating/placeholder” language, link the approving source or asset record in the PR.

## Metadata copy

Titles use `Page · Nari Nuna's Haven` except Home. Descriptions state the page's actual value in plain language and do not claim schedule, services, lore, or partnerships that are not present. Add canonical and social metadata only after a production domain and rights-cleared share image exist.
